Jiva vs cloud AI notetakers

Most AI notetakers send your meeting to their servers, charge a monthly fee, and label everyone else on the call as "Them". Jiva runs on your Mac, is free, and learns who each person is.

Choose Jiva if

  • you want audio and transcripts to stay on your Mac
  • you want it free, with no meeting limit and no account
  • you want real names that stick across every meeting
  • you'd like dictation and to-dos in the same app

Choose a cloud notetaker if

  • you need Windows, a web app or a phone app
  • your team wants shared folders and admin controls
  • you want slightly fewer missed words in fast cross-talk today
  • you don't mind your meetings living on someone else's servers

Questions

Can Jiva replace my cloud notetaker?

For most Mac users, yes. Jiva records without a bot, writes the summary and action items, and answers questions across past meetings, all on your Mac.

Can I run both?

Yes. They don't interfere with each other.

Does Jiva work on Windows?

No. Jiva needs a Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 14.4 or later.
