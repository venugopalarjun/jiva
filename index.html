Straight answers

The questions people ask before installing.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no paid tier and no account to create.

Does anything go to the cloud?

No, not by default. Recording, transcripts, search, people pages and meeting prep work offline on your Mac. The exceptions are ones you turn on: if you ask an AI app like Claude about your meetings, the passages it reads go to that app, and the optional second brain works through your own Claude account.

Does meeting prep need Claude?

No. Without Claude, Jiva builds each prep on your Mac from your past meetings and to-dos. With the optional second brain on, Claude adds what came up in Slack and email.

Do other people on the call know?

No bot joins the call. Jiva records your Mac's audio like QuickTime does, so telling people is up to you.

What if I share my screen?

Your private notes overlay doesn't appear in screen shares.

Why does Jiva ask for Google Calendar?

Optional, read-only access so Jiva can remind you before a meeting, show today's meetings with their prep, open the call with one click, and name the recording. Your calendar is read on your Mac and never sent to Jiva. Claude sees today's meetings only if you turn on the second brain and allow it. See the privacy policy.
