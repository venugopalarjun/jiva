How to get speaker names in a meeting transcript

A transcript gets names in one of three ways: from the meeting's own participant list, from recording each person separately, or by recognising voices. Apps that record on your computer without a bot have only the third. Without it, everyone else on the call becomes one label.

Why names are hard

Turning speech into words and knowing who said them are two different jobs. The second one is called speaker diarization: splitting the audio into "voice A spoke here, voice B spoke there". It gives you labels, not names. A label becomes a name only when something links that voice to a person.

Way 1: the meeting's participant list

A notetaker that joins the call as a bot can see who the meeting app marks as speaking, so it copies the names from the call. This works well, but it needs the bot in the meeting, and it fails when several people share one room and one microphone.

Way 2: one channel per person

If each person is recorded on a separate track, the name is known from the track. Podcast tools work this way. A normal video call does not give you separate tracks for the other people.

Way 3: recognising voices

An app can keep a small mathematical description of a voice, a voiceprint, and compare each new speaker with the ones it knows. You name a voice once; the app writes the name from then on. This works without a bot and in a shared room, and it carries over from one meeting to the next.

It is not perfect. A new voice needs a few minutes of speech, and two similar voices on poor audio can be confused, so a good app lets you correct a name in one click and learns from the correction.

What to look for

Ask three questions of any app. Does it separate the other people, or call them all "Them"? Do names carry over to the next meeting? And where are the voiceprints kept: a voiceprint is biometric data, so it matters whether it sits on your computer or on a company's server.

Common questions

Why does my transcript say "Them" for everyone?

The app recorded the other side of the call as one stream and did not separate the voices in it. It can tell you from them, but not them from each other.

Can an app name a speaker it has never heard?

Not from the voice alone. It needs one link between the voice and a name: you typing it, the participant list, or someone being addressed by name in the call.

Is a voiceprint personal data?

Yes. In many places a voiceprint counts as biometric data. Prefer an app that keeps it on your own device, and tell the people you record.
