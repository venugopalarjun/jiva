Local vs cloud AI notetakers: what leaves your computer

A cloud notetaker sends your meeting's audio to a company's servers, where it is transcribed and summarised and usually stored. A local notetaker does that work on your own computer. Cloud tools are easier to share with a team and run on any device; local tools keep the recording with you, work offline and need a capable machine.

What a cloud notetaker sends

The audio of the meeting, and from it the transcript and the summary. Most services keep these so you can search them later. Many also pass the text to another company's AI model to write the summary. Read the service's privacy policy for three things: how long audio is kept, whether your data trains models, and which other companies process it.

What "local" should mean

Recording, transcription, speaker separation and the summary all run on your computer, and it still works with the internet off. Be careful with the word: an app can record locally with no bot and still send the audio to a server to be transcribed. Test it with Wi-Fi off.

When cloud is the better choice

Your whole team needs one shared, searchable set of meeting notes; you take meetings on a phone or an older computer; or your company has already approved a vendor and you need its admin controls.

When local is the better choice

You talk about things that should not sit on someone else's server: clients, candidates, health, money, unreleased work. Your company does not allow meeting audio to go to an outside service. You do not want another subscription. Or you want your notes as plain files that outlast any app.

Common questions

Is a notetaker without a bot the same as a local notetaker?

No. "No bot" means nothing joins the call. The audio can still be sent to a server afterwards. Local means the transcript and summary are made on your computer.

Is cloud always less private?

It always means another company holds your meeting. Good services encrypt it and limit who can see it, and that is enough for many teams. It is a question of what you discuss and what your company allows.

Can a local notetaker use a cloud AI model?

Some let you choose to. In Jiva this is optional: if you connect Claude, only the parts of a meeting it reads to answer your question are sent, and only when you ask.
